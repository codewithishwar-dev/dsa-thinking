This repository documents my journey of mastering **Data Structures & Algorithms** by focusing on:

# 💡 Thinking Process

Instead of asking:

> "What is the answer?"

Ask:

- What is the input?
- What is the output?
- What are the constraints?
- Can I reduce the search space?
- Can I preprocess?
- Can I use extra memory?
- Is there a known pattern?
- Can I optimize the complexity?
